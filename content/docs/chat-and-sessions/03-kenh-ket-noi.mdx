# Kenh Ket Noi (Channels)

## Tong quan

GoClaw ho tro 7 kenh nhan tin de ket noi AI agent voi cac nen tang nhan tin pho bien. Moi kenh hoat dong doc lap. Moi user tren moi kenh co session rieng biet.

---

## 7 kenh ho tro

| Kenh | Kieu ket noi | Nhom | DM | Streaming |
|------|--------------|------|----|-----------|
| Telegram | Long polling | Co | Co | Typing indicator |
| Discord | Gateway events | Co | Co | Edit "Thinking..." |
| Slack | Socket Mode (WebSocket) | Co | Co | Edit "Thinking..." |
| Feishu/Lark | WebSocket / Webhook | Co | Co | Streaming card |
| Zalo OA | Long polling | Khong | Co | Khong |
| Zalo Personal | Giao thuc noi bo (khong chinh thuc) | Co | Co | Khong |
| WhatsApp | WebSocket bridge ngoai | Co | Co | Khong |

---

## Huong dan su dung tung kenh

### Telegram

- **DM**: Nhan tin truc tiep voi bot — duoc xu ly ngay lap tuc.
- **Nhom**: Bot phai duoc `@mention` truoc khi tra loi (mac dinh). Cac tin nhan truoc khi mention duoc giu lai lam nguyen canh (toi da 50 tin).
- **Reply ngam**: Reply vao tin nhan cua bot trong nhom duoc coi la mention.
- **Voice/Audio**: Tin nhan giong noi duoc phien am qua STT proxy, noi dung duoc tien to `[audio: filename] Transcript: ...` truoc khi gui den agent.
- **Forum topics**: Moi topic trong supergroup co session va cau hinh rieng.
- **Lenh bot**: `/stop` huy run hien tai, `/stopall` huy tat ca, `/reset` xoa lich su, `/help` hien tro giup.

### Discord

- **DM**: Nhan tin truc tiep voi bot.
- **Kenh**: Bot can quyen `GuildMessages` + `MessageContent`; can duoc `@mention` (mac dinh).
- **Streaming**: Bot gui tin nhan "Thinking..." roi chinh sua khi co ket qua.
- **Gioi han**: 2,000 ky tu moi tin nhan — tu dong chia nho neu vuot qua.
- **Typing indicator**: Keepalive 9 giay trong khi agent xu ly.

### Slack

- **DM**: Nhan tin truc tiep voi bot.
- **Kenh**: Can duoc `@mention`; sau khi bot reply trong thread, cac tin nhan tiep theo trong thread do tu dong kich hoat khong can mention (TTL 24 gio).
- **Socket Mode**: Ket noi WebSocket — khong can public URL.
- **Streaming**: Edit-in-place via `chat.update`, throttle 1000ms (Slack Tier 3).
- **Reactions**: Emoji trang thai tren tin nhan nguoi dung (dang suy nghi, dang dung tool, hoan thanh, loi).
- **Gioi han**: 4,000 ky tu moi tin nhan.

### Feishu/Lark

- **DM**: Nhan tin truc tiep voi bot.
- **Nhom**: Can `@mention` (mac dinh); co the tat requirement nay theo tung kenh.
- **Streaming card**: Response duoc hien thi trong interactive card voi hieu ung in chu dong thoi (throttle 100ms).
- **Topic session mode**: Khi bat, moi thread co session rieng biet (session key: `{chatID}:topic:{rootMsgId}`).
- **Media**: Nhan/gui anh, file, audio (mac dinh toi da 30 MB).
- **Ket noi**: WebSocket (mac dinh) hoac Webhook — co the gan tren cong HTTP cua gateway.

### Zalo OA (Official Account)

- Chi ho tro DM — khong co nhom.
- Gioi han 2,000 ky tu moi tin nhan.
- Policy DM mac dinh: `pairing` (yeu cau xac thuc bang code).
- Ho tro gui anh (toi da 5 MB).

### Zalo Personal

- Su dung giao thuc khong chinh thuc (reverse-engineered).
- Ho tro ca DM lan nhom.
- Policy mac dinh: `allowlist` (chi chap nhan nguoi dung trong danh sach cho phep).
- Canh bao: Tai khoan co the bi Zalo khoa bat ky luc nao do vi pham dieu khoan.

---

## Pairing flow (xac thuc ket cap)

Ap dung khi kenh su dung policy `pairing` cho DM:

1. Nguoi dung moi gui tin nhan lan dau — bot tra ve huong dan va **ma 8 ky tu** (hieu luc 60 phut).
2. Nguoi dung cung cap ma nay cho admin.
3. Admin phe duyet qua `device.pair.approve` (Web UI hoac WebSocket RPC).
4. Ket noi duoc ket cap — cac tin nhan tiep theo duoc xu ly binh thuong.

Dac diem ma ket cap:
- Do dai: 8 ky tu
- Bo chu cai: `ABCDEFGHJKLMNPQRSTUVWXYZ23456789` (loai bo ky tu de nham nhu 0, O, 1, I, L)
- Toi da 3 yeu cau cho cung mot tai khoan
- Debounce: bot khong gui lai huong dan qua 1 lan moi 60 giay

## Chinh sach ket noi (Channel Policies)

### DM Policies

| Policy | Hanh vi |
|--------|---------|
| `open` | Chap nhan tin nhan tu bat ky ai |
| `allowlist` | Chi chap nhan nguoi gui co trong danh sach cho phep |
| `pairing` | Yeu cau xac thuc ket cap truoc khi xu ly |
| `disabled` | Tu choi tat ca DM |

### Group Policies

| Policy | Hanh vi |
|--------|---------|
| `open` | Chap nhan tin nhan tu bat ky nhom |
| `allowlist` | Chi chap nhan nhom co trong danh sach |
| `disabled` | Khong xu ly tin nhan nhom |

## Giao dien quan ly kenh

Route: `/channels`
Nhom Sidebar: Ket Noi
Quyen truy cap: Admin

Hien thi danh sach phan trang cac kenh voi chi bao trang thai (truc tuyen / ngoai tuyen / dang ket noi) va o tim kiem.

**Thao tac:**
- **Tao kenh** — trinh huong dan (cac buoc khac nhau theo loai kenh):
  - Buoc 1: Key (slug), Ten hien thi, Loai kenh, Agent, Thong tin xac thuc, Cau hinh, Bat/tat
  - Buoc 2: Xac thuc (QR code, OAuth, v.v.) — co the bo qua
  - Buoc 3: Cai dat nhom/chu de — co the bo qua
- **Chinh sua kenh** — cap nhat cau hinh
- **Xoa kenh** — nhap chinh xac ten kenh de xac nhan (bi chan voi kenh mac dinh)
- **Xac thuc lai** — chay lai luong xac thuc (vi du: Zalo QR)
- **Xem chi tiet kenh** — dieu huong den trang chi tiet

**Cai dat nang cao kenh**: Mang, Gioi han, Streaming, Hanh vi, Kiem soat truy cap.

Admin co the bat/tat kenh qua `channels.toggle` (yeu cau quyen admin).

---

## Luu y

- Moi kenh gan voi mot agent duy nhat — neu muon nhieu agent, tao nhieu kenh.
- Thay doi agent cua kenh anh huong den tat ca tin nhan moi (session cu van dung agent cu).
- Zalo Personal khong phai kenh chinh thuc — su dung chiu rui ro. Nen dung Zalo OA cho muc dich san xuat.
- WhatsApp yeu cau WebSocket bridge ngoai — lien he nha cung cap de cai dat.
